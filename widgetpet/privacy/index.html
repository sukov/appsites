Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

Widget Pet does not collect, transmit or store any personal data. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs in the app. The app has no server and makes no network requests of its own.

What stays on your device

The following is stored only on your iPhone, in storage shared between the app and its widget. It is removed when you delete the app:

  • Your pet: its name, needs, growth and streak.
  • Your progress: coins, outfits, shells, game scores and settings such as bedtime.

None of this is uploaded, backed up to a service run by the developer, or shared with anyone.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your payment details or Apple Account. The app only learns which items you own so it can unlock them.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders such as "your pet is hungry" are scheduled on your iPhone. They are local notifications. Nothing is sent from a server, and you can turn them off in Settings at any time.

Children

Widget Pet collects no data from anyone, including children, and contains no ads. Purchases go through Apple's standard confirmation, so parents can use Ask to Buy and Screen Time to control them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open a request and it will be answered there.
